Task-specific excerpt only. No full Notification Center capture is committed: it would
contain the user's unrelated notifications (same rule as research/issue-59-desktop-alerts).

Observation method: OVRCR delivers alerts as `osascript -e 'display notification ...'`
and the sound as `afplay /System/Library/Sounds/Glass.aiff`
(crates/ovrcr-tui/src/dashboard/desktop.rs host_command / the Glass path). A 30 ms process
sampler recorded each dispatch with its argv. This records DISPATCH, not display: a
successful host command does not prove the banner was drawn or the sound audible --
Focus/Do Not Disturb, notification permission and output volume decide that, and macOS may
silently suppress osascript notifications. That caveat is the same one issue-59 recorded.
