## Exact unresolved decision and next action

The owner must obtain and record the applicable license/use interpretation for this operation: **a macOS app distributed with a sealed `Contents/Resources/Glass.aiff` symlink, resolving only to the user's existing `/System/Library/Sounds/Glass.aiff`, requests that sound through UserNotifications while running licensed macOS on Apple-branded hardware, without shipping, extracting, converting or copying the audio.** Determine how the applicable acquisition terms, §§1A/C and 2A/B/J/N, any separate agreement and jurisdictional supplement govern that use. Seek qualified interpretation or Apple clarification if needed; signing, playback and an API recipe cannot supply that decision.

Keep #221's Glass gate open until that decision is evidenced. No unsupported sound substitution or independent player is proposed. First-use permission and Settings portability remain separate #221 blockers. Once the gate is resolved, later release validation must verify that packaging preserves the link and supported systems retain a readable target; existing single-host evidence does not satisfy those later checks.
