▣ OVRCR GUI

03 · Sections instead of a tree. Chevrons are gone. Projects become upper-case section headers with a rule to the edge and a blank line before them; workspaces get a branch glyph and bold; sessions are a plain indented list. Depth is carried by typography and whitespace, which is easier to scan than four indent steps that differ by two cells. Selection is a left bar plus a lighter background, so the status and provider colours stay visible on the selected row. Fold by clicking a header; folded rows show a count.
